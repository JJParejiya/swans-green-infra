— FAQ

Quietly answered, in plain language.

If your question isn't here, our principal designer will gladly take it personally — over a phone call or, better still, on the model floor.

What configurations are currently available across Swansgreen residences?

Our active inventory ranges from compact 2 BHK apartments at Abhiram City to 4 BHK riverfront residences at Parwati Heights. Vrundavan Dham offers boutique 2 & 3 BHK towers, and Gokuldham City offers freehold plots from 1,200 to 3,600 sqft.

Is RERA approval in place for every project?

Yes. Every Swansgreen residence is fully RERA-registered. Our umbrella registration number is BRERAP194328061225250932E00, and project-specific registration is shared with you at booking and visible on every brochure.

Which banks are pre-approved for home loans on these projects?

SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, LIC Housing Finance and PNB Housing have all approved one or more of our residences. Our booking desk can introduce you to a relationship manager and walk you through indicative EMIs.

When can I expect possession of a unit booked today?

Abhiram City is move-in ready today. Vrundavan Dham hands over from late 2025. Parwati Heights has a committed possession of December 2026. Each timeline is contractually backed by our RERA filing.

Can the unit be customised — finishes, layout, joinery?

Within structural limits, yes. If you book before the slab on your floor is cast, our principal designer can rework non-load-bearing walls, change the bath layout, switch your wood palette, or upgrade specific finishes. Customisation requests are best raised at booking.

Do you assist NRI buyers with documentation and remote site visits?

Yes — we routinely close NRI bookings end-to-end. We arrange POA-based documentation, video walk-throughs of the model home, and curated site visits when you are in town. Payments accept NRE / NRO routing through our partner banks.

What is included in the booking amount and the payment schedule?

Booking is a non-refundable token of 5% of the agreement value. Subsequent payments follow a construction-linked plan defined per project. There are no hidden charges — registration, GST and statutory dues are itemised upfront in the cost sheet shared at booking.

Begin where every great residence begins — with a quiet visit.

Schedule a private walk-through with our principal designer. We will show you the model home, the material palette, and the floor plates we believe match your brief. No commitments — just a considered first conversation.
