patna's first true riverfront luxury address.

parwati heights occupies a rare strip of land on the bank of the ganga — close enough to the river to read the changing weather on the water, far enough from the road to keep the city quiet. it is the kind of plot that comes once in a generation, and we have spent eighteen months thinking about how to honour it.

the brief to aryan architects was simple: build something that would still feel right in fifty years. the result is two slender towers oriented for cross-ventilation, lifted off a landscaped podium, and bound by a single private promenade that runs uninterrupted from the lobby to the river edge.

an address measured in light, not metres.

parwati heights sits on the riverfront stretch of s p verma road extension — five minutes from patna junction, eight from jp narayan international airport, and walking distance from the city's best healthcare and retail. but its real luxury is its quiet: it faces the river, not the road.

the plot has been preserved largely as-is. mature peepal and banyan trees have been retained inside the boundary. the promenade is shared with no through traffic.

— faq · parwati heights

quietly answered, in plain language.

our principal designer is happy to take any further questions personally — over a phone call, or, better still, on the model floor.

when is possession of parwati heights scheduled?

committed possession is december 2026, contractually backed by our rera filing. construction is currently on schedule — we publish a quarterly progress note for registered eois.

what configurations and carpet areas are on offer?

two slender g+21 towers house just 64 residences in total — 3 bhk from 1,850 sqft and 4 bhk up to 2,980 sqft. every flat is corner-facing or river-facing; there are no internal-facing units.

is the project rera-approved?

yes — our rera registration number is brerap194328061225250932e00, visible on every brochure and at the project site.

what is the starting price and the payment plan?

starting price is ₹ 1.65 cr for the smallest 3 bhk. booking is a 5% non-refundable token; subsequent milestones follow a construction-linked plan. a full cost sheet (registration, gst, statutory dues itemised) is shared at booking.

which banks are pre-approved for home loans?

sbi, hdfc, icici and lic housing finance have all approved parwati heights. we can introduce you to a relationship manager for indicative emis and pre-sanction.

can finishes or layouts be customised?

within structural limits — yes. if your floor's slab has not yet been cast, our principal designer can rework non-load-bearing walls, alter the bath layout, change the wood palette, or upgrade specific finishes. request these changes at booking.

do you assist nri buyers with documentation?

yes. we arrange poa-based documentation, video walk-throughs of the model home, and curated site visits when you are in town. payments are accepted via nre / nro routing through our partner banks.
